A Trading 212 portfolio tracker is a separate tool that reads your Trading 212 data, from a CSV export or an API connection, and shows what the app doesn't: dividend totals, realised profit per stock, fees paid and how you trade. The main options are Vizvest (free, no account, CSV only), Sharesight, Snowball Analytics, Getquin and AllInvestView.

---

## Why do Trading 212 investors use a portfolio tracker?

Because the app is built for buying and selling, not for analysis. It shows your current value and the return on each holding, but not the totals you need to understand your results over time.

Trading 212 is a big platform: in May 2025 it said it had passed [£25 billion in client assets and around 4.5 million clients](https://www.trading212.com/legal-documentation/Trading-212-Press-release-21.05.2025_EN.pdf). Many of those investors want answers the app doesn't give directly:

- How much have I received in dividends this year, and from which stocks?
- What profit or loss did I actually lock in on the shares I sold?
- How much have currency conversion fees and withholding tax cost me?
- When do I trade most, and how often do my sells end in profit?

All of that is in your [Trading 212 CSV export](/articles/how-to-export-trading-212-data). A tracker turns it into figures and charts.

## What does Vizvest show for Trading 212?

Vizvest is a free, CSV-based analyser built only for Trading 212 Invest and Stocks ISA exports. You [upload your Trading 212 export to Vizvest](/dashboard), several files at once if you have years of history, and it builds the dashboard in your browser.

**Best for:** Trading 212 investors who want to understand their realised results, income and costs without creating an account or handing over their data.

What you get:

- **Portfolio overview:** buy and sell volume, realised profit, dividends, currency conversion fees, deposits and allocation by holding.
- **Realised profit per position**, taken from Trading 212's own **Result** figures.
- **Costs:** currency conversion fees and withholding tax added up.
- **Dividends:** by month and quarter, year over year and by stock, net of tax withheld at source.
- **Per-stock pages** with the full buy, sell and dividend history of any holding.
- **Trading activity:** a day-by-day heatmap, streaks, your busiest month and your win rate.
- **Multi-currency:** every row converted to your base currency at the rate in your CSV, pence included.
- **Partial data warnings** when sells outrun buys, a sign that your export window is too short.

What it doesn't do: Vizvest has no price feed, so it does not show today's market value, unrealised gains, yield on cost or benchmark comparisons. It does not connect to your Trading 212 account. Nothing is uploaded or stored, and refreshing the page clears your data.

Vizvest doesn't cover tax. For anything tax-related, speak to a qualified tax adviser.

## How does Sharesight work with Trading 212?

Sharesight connects to Trading 212 automatically or takes a CSV, and is strongest on performance and income reporting.

**Best for:** investors who want automatic syncing, live prices and dividend income reports across several brokers.

According to its [Trading 212 partner page](https://www.sharesight.com/partners/trading-212/), Sharesight syncs through SnapTrade using your Trading 212 API key, imports new trades daily, and can pull in past trades at setup. The integration imports standard buys and sells only. You can also upload Trading 212 CSV exports.

## How does Snowball Analytics work with Trading 212?

Snowball Analytics is a dividend-focused tracker with a free plan and paid tiers. You import your Trading 212 broker report, and paid plans add automatic broker sync.

**Best for:** dividend investors who want live prices and multi-broker tracking.

Snowball's [free plan](https://snowball-analytics.com/portfolio-tracker) covers one portfolio with up to 10 holdings, including dividend tracking and broker report import. Paid tiers (Starter, Investor and Expert) raise the limits and add features. If you hold more than 10 positions, expect to need a paid plan.

## How does Getquin work with Trading 212?

Getquin is a portfolio tracker with a social community feed. Its help centre lists [manual import](https://help.getquin.com/en/articles/6996559-trading212) as the method for Trading 212.

**Best for:** investors who want a tracker with a community feed and don't mind importing files.

You upload your Trading 212 export and review the records before confirming. There is a free plan, with paid Premium and Wealth tiers for more analysis. For Trading 212, plan on importing files rather than a live connection.

## What can I calculate myself from my Trading 212 export?

Anything a CSV-only tool can't show, you can work out in a spreadsheet. Two examples:

**Unrealised gain.** Take the shares you still hold, multiply by today's price, and subtract what you paid. If you hold 40 shares bought for a total of £1,000 and the price is now £30, the holding is worth £1,200, an unrealised gain of £200.

**Yield on cost.** Divide the last 12 months of dividends per share by your average purchase price. If you paid £25 a share on average and received £1.50 a share in dividends over the year, your yield on cost is 6%.

Vizvest gives you the inputs for both: your buys per stock on the per-stock page and your dividend totals by stock. It also shows the costs that eat into returns. For a full breakdown of those, see [Trading 212 fees explained](/articles/trading-212-fees-explained).

## How do I start tracking my Trading 212 portfolio with Vizvest?

Export your history, upload it and open the dashboard. It takes a few minutes.

1. **Export your Trading 212 CSV.** Go to **Menu → History** and tap the export icon. Choose up to 12 months per export and repeat for each period of your history. The [step-by-step export guide](/articles/how-to-export-trading-212-data) has the details.
2. **Upload it.** Go to [the Vizvest dashboard](/dashboard) and drop in your CSV files. Upload them all at once; duplicates are removed automatically.
3. **Explore.** Your portfolio overview, dividends, per-stock history and trading activity load straight away.

No account, no email, no password. Your data stays on your device.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free portfolio tracker for Trading 212?

It depends on what you need. Vizvest is free with no account and reads your Trading 212 CSV in the browser for realised profit, dividends, fees and trading activity. Sharesight, Snowball Analytics and Getquin have free plans with limits and add live prices, but you need an account and paid plans unlock more.

### Is Vizvest free?

Yes. Vizvest is free and needs no account. You upload your Trading 212 CSV export and the dashboard is built in your browser straight away.

### Is my data safe with Vizvest?

Vizvest processes your CSV entirely in your browser. The file is never uploaded to or stored on a server, and it is cleared when you refresh or close the page.

### Does Vizvest show unrealised gains or live prices?

No. Vizvest works only from your CSV export and has no price feed, so it shows realised profit, dividends, fees and activity rather than today's market value. Trackers with a price feed, such as Sharesight or Snowball Analytics, show unrealised gains.

### Do I need to re-upload my CSV every time?

Yes. Vizvest keeps nothing between sessions, so upload your latest export whenever you want updated figures. This is a deliberate privacy choice.

### Can I upload several Trading 212 CSV files to Vizvest at once?

Yes. Each Trading 212 export covers at most 12 months, so drop in one file per period to see your full history. Vizvest merges the files and removes duplicate rows, and it warns you if sells outrun buys, which usually means an export is missing.
